\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\subsection{LLM-driven evolutionary program search}

FunSearch \cite{romera2024funsearch} established that pairing an LLM
mutation operator with a programmatic evaluator can produce new
mathematical constructions (cap sets, bin-packing heuristics).
AlphaEvolve \cite{novikov2025alphaevolve} generalized the recipe to
whole codebases and reported results across more than fifty
mathematical problems --- rediscovering the best known construction
in ${\sim}75\%$ of them and improving it in ${\sim}20\%$, a framing
that makes \emph{rediscovery rate} an accepted currency of this
literature and one we adopt. Follow-on systems refine the search
architecture: ShinkaEvolve \cite{shinka2025} targets sample
efficiency (circle packing, Heilbronn triangles, autocorrelation
inequalities), CodeEvolve \cite{codeevolve2025} provides an open
implementation, and Nagda et al.\ apply reinforced variants to Ramsey
and Zarankiewicz numbers \cite{nagda2026ramsey,zarankiewicz2026llm},
explicitly reporting recovery of known bounds alongside improvements.
Negative results have also begun to appear (bijection discovery with
OpenEvolve remaining hard \cite{bijection2025openevolve}), which we
take as a healthy norm and follow in Sections~\ref{sec:benchmark}
and~\ref{sec:limitations}. Analyses of \emph{why} these loops work
concentrate on the evolutionary component \cite{zhang2024understanding};
the contribution of specific \emph{model capabilities} --- in
particular extended reasoning --- has, to our knowledge, not been
isolated experimentally in a discovery loop. Across all published
problem lists of this lineage we find no covering-design instances;
both gaps are addressed here.

\subsection{Reasoning-mode ablations}

Controlled comparisons of LLMs with reasoning enabled versus disabled
exist outside discovery loops, with strikingly mixed outcomes:
reasoning helps handwriting-synthesis agents \cite{handwriting2026}
and long-form information-control tasks \cite{pic2025}, is neutral
for prompt-attack detection \cite{promptattack2026}, and
\emph{degrades} content-moderation accuracy \cite{moderation2026}.
This task-dependence is the backdrop against which our result should
be read: in the structural-paradigm-shift regime of
Section~\ref{sec:experiment}, the effect is not a few points of
accuracy but a categorical 3/3-versus-0/3 separation with a 36\%
objective improvement.

\subsection{Covering designs}

Upper bounds for covering numbers $C(v,k,t)$ are curated by the La
Jolla Covering Repository and its live successor \cite{ljcr}. The
founding constructions paper \cite{gordon1995new} combined greedy
methods, finite-geometry constructions (including $\mathrm{AG}(m,p)$
flats --- the family our loop rediscovers), and synthesis rules;
subsequent stochastic-search work (simulated annealing
\cite{nurmela1993upper}, cooperative tabu search
\cite{dai2006cooperative}) improved many small cells. The specific
cells we target have been stable for 15--30 years, and we find no
post-2020 method wave touching them --- a stagnant-frontier setting
that makes exact ties informative even though they set no records.
